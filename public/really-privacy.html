Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Really?! mobile application.

Last updated: July 15, 2026

1. About Really?!

Really?! is an offline Fact or Myth brain game that lets users explore collections of questions, learn surprising facts, track local progress, and maintain a daily streak.

The app does not require an account, login, or online profile.

2. Information Stored Locally

Really?! may store app information locally on your device, including:

  • Questions and collections you have completed
  • Your answers and collection progress
  • Your daily streak
  • Your most recently played collection or session state
  • Collections temporarily unlocked through rewarded advertisements

This information is used only to provide app features and improve your in-app experience. It is not uploaded to our servers because Really?! does not operate a backend service for gameplay data.

3. Advertising

Really?! uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements such as banner ads and rewarded ads.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process certain information, which may include:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted
  • Information about advertisements shown and interactions with them
  • App interaction data
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and performance information

Google may use this information to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements, as well as to prevent fraud and abuse.

Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies and technologies information .

4. Rewarded Ads

Really?! may allow you to voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement to unlock a collection or another optional app feature.

Watching rewarded ads is optional. Free content remains available without watching rewarded advertisements.

5. Consent and Advertising Choices

Depending on your region, the app may show a consent or privacy message that allows you to make choices about advertising personalization and the use of certain identifiers.

You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through your device settings or Google's advertising controls.

If you decline personalized advertising where that choice is available, you may still see non-personalized or limited advertisements.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

Really?! does not require or directly collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • An account password or login credentials
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings, or files
  • Payment card or banking information
  • Health or fitness information

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be collected or processed by third-party services used by the app, such as Google AdMob, for advertising, measurement, diagnostics, security, and fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and streak information may remain stored locally on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Because we do not receive or store this gameplay information on our own servers, we cannot access or remotely delete it.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Really?! is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers. Advertising services may process limited device and advertising information as described in this policy and according to applicable laws and platform requirements.

10. Security

Network communications performed by third-party services such as Google AdMob use encrypted connections in transit. Locally stored app data is protected by the security features of your device and operating system.

11. Third-Party Links

Advertisements displayed in Really?! may link to third-party websites, apps, or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, or applicable legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
