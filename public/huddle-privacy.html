Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Huddle ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the Huddle mobile application.

Huddle is an offline social party game designed for friends to play together on a single device.

2. Information You Enter Into Huddle

During gameplay, you may enter information such as player names and answers to game questions.

Huddle does not require you to create an account and does not upload your player names, saved groups, or game answers to our servers.

Player names and saved groups may be stored locally on your device so that you can reuse them in future game sessions.

Game answers are processed locally on your device for the purpose of running the game and are not sent to our servers.

3. Local Data Storage

Huddle stores certain information locally on your device, including player names and saved groups.

This locally stored information is used only to provide app features, such as allowing you to quickly reuse a group of players in a future game.

We do not have access to this locally stored gameplay information and do not transfer it to our own servers.

4. Advertising

Huddle uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements, including banner and rewarded advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process certain information, which may include:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App interactions and advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Advertising data

Google may use this information for purposes such as providing, delivering, and measuring advertisements, analytics, and preventing fraud and abuse.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's own privacy policies and practices.

5. Rewarded Advertisements

Huddle may allow you to voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for access to certain game content, such as unlocking a game pack.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. You can continue using the available free content without watching a rewarded advertisement.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Huddle does not operate a backend service that receives or stores your player names, saved groups, or private game answers.

Certain information may be collected or processed by third-party services integrated into the app, such as Google AdMob, as described in this Privacy Policy and according to those services' own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

Player names and saved groups may remain stored locally on your device so that they can be reused in future game sessions.

Locally stored information may remain on your device until it is deleted through available app features, the app's data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Game answers are processed locally for gameplay and are not stored on or transmitted to our servers.

Third-party services integrated into Huddle, such as Google AdMob, may retain information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

Huddle is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a way that concerns you, please contact us using the information below.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and device settings, you may have choices regarding advertising personalization and the use of certain device identifiers.

These choices may be available through consent messages shown in the app, your device settings, or Google's advertising controls.

10. Third-Party Services

Huddle uses Google AdMob for advertising. Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

We encourage you to review Google's privacy information to understand how Google processes information when you use apps that integrate Google services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
