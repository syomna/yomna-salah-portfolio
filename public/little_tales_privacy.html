Privacy Policy

App: Little Tales: What If?  |  Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to "Little Tales: What If?" (the "App"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our young users and providing a safe, secure, and delightful environment for children and parents alike.

1. Dedicated to Kids' Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Our App is specifically designed for children. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal information from children.
  • We do not track, profile, or monitor our users.

2. Information Collection and Use

No Personal Data Collected: You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or input any personal details to enjoy the App.

Local-Only Game Progress: Any progress data, completed story levels, or earned stars are stored entirely locally on your device's secure storage. We do not transmit this data to external servers, and we have no access to it.

No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytical tools that monitor your child's in-app behavior.

3. Paid App Structure & Zero Advertisements

"Little Tales: What If?" is a premium, paid application.

  • There are zero advertisements or third-party product placements within the App.
  • There are no hidden in-app purchases or subscription models, ensuring a safe space where children cannot accidentally trigger financial charges.

4. Language Preferences

The App offers English and Arabic language toggles. When you save a language preference, this configuration flag is saved exclusively on your own device's internal local storage cache (SharedPreferences).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or App functionality. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
